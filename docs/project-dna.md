## Purpose & Problem Statement

**Problem**: Users searching for food recipes and restaurant recommendations need a modern platform with comprehensive search, map integration, and Persian localization for Iranian and Dari-speaking markets.

**Solution**: A full-featured web application for food recipes and restaurant recommendations with advanced search, map integration, and Persian/RTL support tailored to regional markets.

---

## Target Users

- **Primary**: Food enthusiasts and home cooks in Persian-speaking regions
- **Secondary**: Restaurant owners and food bloggers managing listings
- **Tertiary**: Recipe app developers and food tech entrepreneurs
